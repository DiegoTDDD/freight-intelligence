# ANTAQ navigation and regional references

The water-mode view adds 43 reviewed reference records from the official
[ANTAQ Desempenho Aquaviário 2025](https://www.gov.br/antaq/pt-br/central-de-conteudos/publicacoes-da-antaq/Anuario2025.pdf)
and its [official annual release](https://www.gov.br/antaq/pt-br/central-de-conteudos/noticias/2026/setor-aquaviario-brasileiro-movimentou-em-2025-1-4-bi-de-toneladas/).

| Table | Records | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Navigation totals | 3 | 2025 port throughput, coastal cabotage and Interior navigation category |
| Leading inland cargo | 10 | Labeled cargo categories from report page 22; not all cargo |
| Coastal state endpoints | 22 | Eleven labeled states, origin/destination separately, in thousand TEU |
| Grain regions | 8 | Arco Norte/rest-of-Brazil maize/soybean movements and exports, 2024/2025 |
| Monthly port handling | 12 | 2025 monthly displayed whole tonnes, both directions, all navigation categories |

The twelve monthly records were read on 2026-11-18 from **Exibir dados** on
GR1.6, object `hxpsVb`, in the [official interactive panel](https://aquarela.antaq.gov.br/single/?appid=56c32604-37bd-4993-9904-1b2c87706808&sheet=bb5c2c57-9e0e-46d4-a2bb-006e1de59add&opt=currsel%2Cctxmenu).
Filters: year 2025 only, no month/geographic/cargo filters, displayed unit tonnes.
The panel labels its edition updated 2026-04-01 08:17. Monthly displayed values sum
to **1,402,731,832 t**, versus its annual headline **1,402,731,833 t**. The one-tonne
display precision discrepancy is preserved; no month receives an invented adjustment.
The annual report's rounded 1,403.1 million t remains a separate edition/reference.
Neither series identifies unique shipments. The export link generated by Qlik
returned unavailable; the accessible data table supplied all twelve labeled rows.

The preparation uses **labeled figures only**. Unlabeled points on the historical
charts are not estimated from pixel positions. The PDF source hash is
`75a258baeb9267d1cf1c0be8b9353aa31850b950b55197e79b9072e484961e2e`.
The reviewed release HTML bytes have a separate hash; changing navigation/site
markup need not mean the underlying published statistic changed.

Coastal state figures are marginal endpoints, not an origin/destination matrix.
The app does not infer links such as SP–AM from these numbers. Unlabeled states
remain unavailable rather than being filled with zeros. TEU is container capacity,
not tonnes; no density or payload conversion is inferred.

Navigation categories, port handling and exports overlap. Their sum is not a
national unique-shipment total. The Interior category differs from all traffic
that happens to use inland waterways, which can also include other navigation
categories. Grain region aggregates do not describe an operational corridor or
provide a navigable itinerary. Regional comparisons retain source rounding.

Rebuild with `python scripts/prepare_brazil_water.py`; optionally pass
`--source-pdf PATH` to require the exact reviewed edition. Then regenerate modal
indicators with `python scripts/prepare_brazil_modes.py`. The 2025 port total is
kept under a separate indicator from the older ONTL series, whose historical
rounding/revisions differ. Raw ANTAQ downloads remain outside the hosted image.

The generic five-mode snapshot includes three additional 2025 water indicators,
alongside the other modal histories. The detailed 55-row table is separately available
in the dashboard, CSV export and `GET /api/brazil/water-freight`, filtered by dataset,
reference year and direction. Reader checks enforce hashes, units, populations,
direction, year coverage and duplicate keys; invalid/missing bundles return 503.

This closes the absence of inland/coastal reference analyses, not complete raw
waterway-flow coverage. Historical monthly records beyond 2025, OD flows outside the reviewed 2025 cabotage population and fuller corridor
detail remain future work. The legacy ANTAQ download page returned 502 during
source discovery; the reviewed official report remains reproducible independently.

## Paired regional cabotage shares

The separate **Regional cabotage origin–destination shares (2025)** view contains
21 original region-to-region pairs from the official panel's **GR 4.5** Sankey
object `wGpRXf`, exported through **Exportar dados** on 2026-11-18.
It is a paired table, unlike the coastal state marginal endpoint indicators above.

The original [6,026-byte XLSX](../deployment/brazil_water/cabotage_region_pairs_2025.xlsx)
is preserved unchanged, SHA-256
`183b4b2a555b0e69fb08553416be9adc31022be9e11d237c3ab6d848ce7a6ab1`.
Its columns are `Região Origem`, `Região Destino`, `Distribuição Percentual`.
The numeric values are fractions (0–1), summing to one within floating-point
tolerance; the dashboard displays them as percentages. The 51.2131250764% link
from `ZEE/Plat. Continental` to `Sudeste` is preserved rather than assigned to an
invented coastal state or port. `Indeterminado` also remains a source category.

The reviewed UI selected **2025 only**, no month, geographic or cargo filter,
and scale `DadosEscala=1`; panel edition updated **2026-04-01 08:17**. The original
XLSX has **no year column**, so period attribution relies on that recorded export
context, the [archived export screenshot](../deployment/brazil_water/cabotage_region_pairs_2025_context.jpg)
showing the year/month controls, and the exact reviewed workbook hash. The reader
rejects a changed filter-period contract. It is not an automatic current-data feed.
The panel's contextual transport headline was 222,975,436 displayed tonnes;
the share view does **not** reconstruct absolute tonnes from that headline or the
separate 303.7-million-tonne coastal-handling reference.

`GET /api/brazil/water-pairs` returns all 21 records. Optional `origin_region` and
`destination_region` select source-region labels. Filters preserve the original
full-panel denominator, with no renormalization. Unlisted pairs return an empty
selection, not fabricated zero-flow records. Only year 2025 is available.

Rebuild offline with `python scripts/prepare_brazil_water_pairs.py`; the exact
reviewed XLSX is bundled, and a changed source fails the hash check. The reader
also validates the calendar, distinct keys, regions, positive fractions and unit
sum. This supplies regional paired analysis, not port-level routes, live vessel
positions, navigable itineraries or complete inland/coastal shipment coverage.
