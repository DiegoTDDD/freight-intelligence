# Historical gas and source-cell diagnostics

The verified gas view now covers 2021–2025: 600 monthly operator/direction
aggregates in total, 120 per year. Each annual subtotal is independently reconstructed from its
twelve monthly files. Receipts and deliveries remain separate; transfers between
operators can repeat the same gas. Neither is unique national supply or production.

The [official ANP source catalogue](https://www.gov.br/anp/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/dados-abertos/dados-consolidados-movimentacao-de-gas-natural-em-gasodutos-de-transporte)
provides the exact annual/monthly links. Historical downloads are discovered from
that catalogue, not generated by guessing names. Every downloaded CSV is hashed.
The reviewed 2022 files use `Realizado (mil m³)` alongside the later
`Volume Realizado (mil m³)` name. This explicit thousand-m³ equivalence is allowed
only for 2022; it does not relax the 2025 contract. The exact unitless 2021 label is separately reviewed against the archived official
dictionary described below; no other unitless labels are accepted.

The additional reviewed 2024 rules are:

- 30 exactly repeated full volume rows are excluded and counted. Conflicting
  duplicate keys still fail validation; no row is selected arbitrarily.
- Ten source rows lack a shipper code but provide a shipper name. The original
  name is used only to distinguish historical rows; no shipper identifier is invented.
- Two negative values with spaces after the minus sign are retained numerically.
- There are 7,734 missing receipt cells and 79,484 missing delivery cells. They
  remain missing. Year comparisons depend on operator/contract coverage and must
  not be presented as changes in unique national supply.

2024 observed subtotals are 44,243.736439 million m³ received and
44,826.157145 million m³ delivered. Their statistical population differs from 2025.

## Investigated 2025 missing values

The 33 absent daily source cells have been traced to exact records and dates:

- GARUVA, TBG, receipt point 622847: all 31 days of January 2025.
- RPBC, NTS, delivery point 222747: 2025-05-31.
- São Bernardo do Campo, NTS, delivery point 222790: 2025-05-31.

The source cells are blank. Their absence does not establish zero flow or an
operational cause. The diagnostics include source URL, SHA-256, record and dates;
the observed subtotals remain unchanged. Original 2025 strict duplicate rejection
is retained by default.

## UI, API and memory

Choose the reference year in the pipeline view and open the missing-values
diagnostic expander. Detailed missing-date lists are grouped losslessly and the
validated historical bundle is shared read-only across sessions, with bounded
cache size and file-version invalidation.

`GET /api/brazil/gas-movements?year=2022` selects history; years 2021–2025 are
accepted and omitting year keeps 2025. Source diagnostics are restricted to the
selected year before operator/month/direction filters, in both UI and API.
`include_diagnostics=true` adds record groups, paginated with `diagnostics_limit`
(maximum 500) and `diagnostics_offset`. Normal responses omit large diagnostic
arrays. Annual references explicitly retain the entire selected-year population
even when month/operator filters narrow the displayed rows.

Rebuild with `python -m scripts.prepare_brazil_gas_history --cache-dir DIRECTORY`.
The reviewed historical years are 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2024; incomplete or incompatible years fail
before replacing the historical output. Raw monthly CSVs stay outside deployment.

## Reviewed 2021 unit contract

The exact 2021 label `Volume Realizado` is accepted only with the archived
[official ANP dictionary](https://www.gov.br/anp/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/dados-abertos/arquivos/arquivos-movimentacao-de-gas-natural-em-gasodutos-de-transporte/metadados-gn-sim.pdf),
version 12 June 2026, page 2: it defines this variable in thousand m³.
The deployed PDF and every 2021 monthly source record carry the pinned SHA-256
`c885e815d1c00048d41e5aecb299bb7ffe44f03bffeb6543fababaf2223ac67e`.
A changed dictionary or missing contract fails validation; other unitless labels
and later years do not gain this exception. The 2021 observed receipt/delivery
subtotals are 64,104.127740 / 61,563.940370 million m³. Missing source cells
remain missing, and 72 named rows without shipper codes remain disclosed.
The previously reviewed 2022–2024 monthly values are unchanged.
